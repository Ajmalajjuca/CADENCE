## Tone Calibration Summary

| Dimension | Your voice |
|---|---|
| Formality | Casual, conversational |
| Sentence length | Short and punchy, one idea per line |
| Opinion strength | Clear takes (Mode C by default until stories are added), often delivered through sarcasm |
| Lowercase vs sentence case | All lowercase, including "i". Acronyms and product names keep their caps (AI, LLM, API) |
| Emoji / punctuation | A few emojis for energy; text emoticons like `:)` for a dry, sarcastic sign-off |
| Humor | Sarcastic, playful. Takes a corporate cliché literally and runs with it (e.g. "we're a family" → bring groceries) |
| Signature words/phrases | Open letter format ("dear manager,"); deadpan closers ("you said it. i'm just respecting the company culture.") |
| Stories vs frameworks | Opinions and practical lessons for now; no stories in the bank yet |

**Why it works:** sarcastic take on fake workplace culture

dear manager,

before casually saying “we’re a family” in the workplace, please understand the responsibilities that come with it.

you may be required to:

-bring groceries to my house
-clean the entire house
-cook for the family
-occasionally handle my emotional damage
and by my family, i obviously mean our family.

you said it.
i’m just respecting the company culture.

please act like the responsible family member you claimed to be. :)
