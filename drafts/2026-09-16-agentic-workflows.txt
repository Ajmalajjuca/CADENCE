your startup doesn't need an autonomous AI agent.
it needs a flowchart with an LLM in 2 of the boxes.

i know. "flowchart" doesn't sound like a series A pitch.

but here's the math nobody puts in the demo:

say your agent gets each step right 95% of the time. great, right?

chain 10 steps → 59% success.
chain 20 steps → 36%.

that's not autonomy. that's a coin toss with a nice UI. 🪙

gartner expects 40%+ of agentic AI projects to get cancelled by 2027.
my read: most of them let the model decide things a plain if-statement could've handled.

the AI tools that actually ship look boring:

→ the steps are fixed in code
→ the LLM only makes the calls that need judgment
→ anything destructive waits for a human to click yes

even anthropic's guide on building agents says: start with the simplest thing that works. add autonomy only when you actually need it.

so next time a dev shop pitches you an "autonomous agent", ask one thing:
which decisions does the model actually make?

if the answer is "all of them"… good luck with that. :)

what's one task in your startup you'd trust AI to run start to finish? 👇
